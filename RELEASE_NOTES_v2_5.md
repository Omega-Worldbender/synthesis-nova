## 🧪 Field-tested, not guessed

Five rounds, 26 fresh agents, Opus and Haiku tiers. These are the author's field tests, not benchmarks. 📊

```
  ✅ the work stays correct ............ 100% of automated tests, every arm
  🔥 register held (Opus, with CORE) ... 4 of 4  vs  1 of 4 without
  🗣️ problems raised at TURN 2 ......... instead of buried at the end
  🔍 peer review between agents ........ caught 4 holes + 1 overclaim,
                                         with a reproduction, before
                                         anything reached the overseer
  ⚡ refresh as its own turn ........... 4 of 4 landed
     refresh buried inside a task ...... 0 of 4
  🌮 bonus: agents that answered in the user's language held the
     register longest
```

*Honest line, on its own:* CORE didn't make the code more correct. Agents were already good at that. It made them **better to work with**: they stay in the register you set, speak up early, check each other, and tell you where the noise nudged them. 💎
